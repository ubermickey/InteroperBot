Start the WhatsApp Bridge

The bridge is a local Node.js process that connects to WhatsApp. Start it from your terminal:

python bot.py --setup-whatsapp click to copy

Or with a phone number for pairing code (instead of QR):

python bot.py --setup-whatsapp +1XXXXXXXXXX click to copy

Scan QR Code

  2. iOS: Settings → Linked Devices — Android: Menu (⋮) → Linked Devices

  4. Point your camera at the QR code below

Next Steps

  • Press Ctrl+C in the terminal to stop the setup bridge
  • Run the bot with WhatsApp enabled:
WHATSAPP_ENABLED=true python bot.py click to copy
  • Auth credentials saved to helpers/wa-bridge/auth/ (persists across restarts)
  • No need to scan QR again unless you unlink the device

Connection Issue

The bridge encountered an error.

If you've been rate-limited, wait 5 minutes before retrying. Delete the auth/ folder for a fresh start:

rm -rf helpers/wa-bridge/auth && python bot.py --setup-whatsapp click to copy
  • QR codes expire every ~20 seconds. WhatsApp generates new ones automatically — this page just displays them.
  • The bridge allows up to 10 reconnect attempts with 5-second delays between each.
  • If you hit "logged out" or repeated failures, wait at least 5 minutes before starting a new setup.
  • Delete helpers/wa-bridge/auth/ only when starting completely fresh — it clears saved credentials.
  • This page never triggers QR generation — it passively displays what the bridge provides.
  • Keep WhatsApp open on your phone during the entire setup process.
